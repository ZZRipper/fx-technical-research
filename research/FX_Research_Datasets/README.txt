FX RESEARCH DATASETS

This archive contains the two untouched supplied Excel workbooks, the available
original notebook snapshots for provenance, 18 cleaned/exported datasets, and the
inventory, dictionary, coverage notes and full-project data manifest.

Start with data/README_Data_Dictionary.txt and data/catalog/dataset_inventory.csv.
Prices are normalized to USD per unit of foreign currency. The main FX panel
covers 2000-01-03 to 2026-05-29. The Federal Reserve H.10 extension is supplied as
a separate source; it must not be silently spliced into the main price series.

The full-project data_manifest.json also lists frozen research results. Those
frozen_files are deliberately absent from this dataset-only archive. They are
included in FX_Research_Complete_Package.zip, together with all notebook runtime
code, frozen engines and reproduction inputs.

To execute the final notebook, use the complete research package. This dataset
archive is intended for data inspection or independent analysis.

DATASET_ARCHIVE_MANIFEST.json records the files actually included here.
