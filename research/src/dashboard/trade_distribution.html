Gross profit is the sum of winning-trade PnL. “Net PnL excluding top winners” subtracts those profits from this closed-trade cohort only; it does not rerun the strategy or identify removable trades in advance. A concentrated profit profile does not by itself invalidate a trend strategy.

Trade details
Accounting & cohort definitions

Trade PnL = $100,000 × the sum of its daily strategy returns. Direction is long or short foreign currency against USD. Holding days are earned FX return intervals, not calendar days. Original execution and exit rules are unchanged.

Win rate = winning completed trades / all eligible completed trades, including flat trades. Average loss is an absolute amount. Payoff = average win / absolute average loss. A dash means undefined. Flat tolerance matches the main explorer: ±0.0000000001 percentage points of trade return.

Cross-window entries are completed trades in this cohort that opened before the selected start; their entire trade PnL is included and marked in the ledger. Open at end counts positions entered by the selected end that have not yet closed then, including positions that close later in the full dataset. Their PnL is excluded from the distribution. Trades with no earned return interval are excluded and counted separately.

The main PnL Explorer sums daily PnL within the date window. This module groups entire trades by exit date. The totals can differ because of pre-window earnings and positions still open at the end. No future part of an open trade is included in this cohort.
