Definitions, execution and evidence limits

Universe: 5 MR rules in 2 families; 12 Trend rules in 6 families. Ensemble, regime-switch and economic-filter versions are excluded. Rules were already examined historically; the universe is not a preregistered, untouched test set.

Accounting: fixed initial USD 100,000, signed 1× exposure, additive daily spot PnL; zero transaction costs, zero financing / carry, zero cash interest. Results are gross spot diagnostics, not deployable net returns. Equal notional is not equal risk.

Timing: existing engine: signal at day t close, execute at next available close, first earned return ends one trading day later. Continuous accounts are sliced, not restarted; carry-in positions contribute to window PnL. Early prices warm indicators.

Sharpe: √252 × mean(daily strategy return) / sample standard deviation. Flat days included, zero reference return. Conventional annualization is descriptive; serial dependence is not adjusted and no significance is claimed. Zero volatility gives an undefined Sharpe.

Drawdown: most negative decline from the running peak of window-reset additive equity, divided by initial capital, in percentage points. Not the same as peak-equity percentage drawdown.

Closed trades: exits inside the window, at least one earned return interval; includes trades entered before the window. Open trades are excluded from this count but contribute daily PnL. Exposure counts held return intervals, including zero-price-change days.

Category summaries: median within each family, then median across families. Metrics summarize individual rules; they are not performance of a combined account. Positive-family breadth uses median cumulative return, while ranking uses median Sharpe; the two may disagree.

Dependence: same-price indicators, overlapping positions, USD exposure and different currency pairs are correlated. Bollinger MR / Breakout have opposite directions under the existing matched rules. More positive rules do not constitute more independent evidence.

Conclusions: qualitative, pair-specific assessments of the displayed fixed-window evidence, not a fitted classification or a claim of statistical significance. No universal numerical cutoff assigns a preferred style. A mixed conclusion may coexist with a small category-median lead.

Interpretation: no fitted cutoffs, p-values, significance labels or “suitable for trading” classification. Earlier-history agreement, annual consistency and family-removal sensitivity are descriptive checks. Existing full-history review prevents calling 2020 onward an untouched OOS test. Costs, portfolio exposure and genuinely unseen validation remain unresolved.

Does the result depend on a few profitable periods?

Period attribution method

All 17 fixed rules and all nine pairs use the original continuous BASE accounts. Daily PnL is assigned by its return-ending date. Trades spanning months are split across those months by daily PnL; a whole trade is not assigned to its exit month. Open positions and carry-ins contribute when held.

Labels are based on fixed, non-overlapping calendar months or quarters. Gain: positive net PnL; Loss: negative net PnL; No exposure: no held return intervals; Active flat: exposure exists but net PnL is zero within a numerical tolerance of USD 0.0000001. Small gains and losses remain gains and losses; no economic or significance cutoff is fitted. Offset measures cancellation continuously instead of imposing a subjective sideways label.

Partial periods are excluded only from the profitable-period concentration comparison, not from the PnL curve or period ledger. Completeness checks the first and last weekdays of the calendar bucket; weekends are adjusted, unavailable weekday boundaries are conservatively treated as incomplete. Exposure is measured from actual held positions, not indicator targets or entry counts. Long = long the foreign currency versus USD; Short = short the foreign currency versus USD. Long and Short percentages both divide by all quote intervals in the selected period; their sum equals Total exposure. Flat = no position and completes 100%. Counts include unchanged-price days. The entry close earns no return interval; the exit close includes the final held interval. Displayed percentages are rounded independently; use the underlying counts or CSV for exact reconciliation.

All figures are gross spot PnL on fixed initial USD 100,000, without transaction costs or carry. Calendar labels are retrospective outcomes, not market regimes or forward trading signals. The export records the last return date when each label is known; do not use that label as a predictor of returns inside the same period.
